Security Volunteer Guidelines & Protocols

  • Shift Capacity: Each security shift slot can accommodate a maximum of 4 registered volunteers.
  • Punctuality: Please arrive at your designated facility (Kingdom Hall or Bunk House) at least 15 minutes before your shift schedule starts.
  • Auto-Reset Mechanism: Confirmed shift records automatically refresh and cycle back to vacant status after 24 hours to prepare for the next operational day.
  • Assistance: For schedule modifications or cancellations, please coordinate directly with the system administrator.
